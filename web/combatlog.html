Data & terms

Shots count fired cartridges. Shot accuracy counts a cartridge as a hit when at least one projectile hits a player or bot. A shotgun blast counts once, regardless of how many pellets hit.

Projectile / pellet accuracy is a separate detail statistic; contacts are damage events and one projectile can create several. Old reports without cartridge counters cannot supply shot counts, shot accuracy or corrected ammunition costs.

Body damage is after armour. Body damage prevented is the difference between EFT's pre- and post-armour values.

Ricochet uses EFT's deflection flag. “Head area” identifies a body region, not a specific helmet or plate.

The mannequin shows schematic collider zones, never exact impact points, shot directions, movement, or positions.
